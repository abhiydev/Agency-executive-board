## 5. Indian Commercial Terms & Payment Terms

### Payment Structure
- **Single-Page Landing Pages (₹12k–₹15k):** 50% Advance Deposit upon contract signoff, 50% upon final launch handover.
- **Multi-Page & Custom Projects:** 40% Advance Deposit ➔ 30% Staging Demo Review ➔ 30% Pre-Launch Handover.

### Statutory & Financial Rules
- **GST Exclusions:** All prices quoted are exclusive of 18% GST, itemized on official tax invoices.
- **Accepted Payment Modes:** UPI (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm), NEFT / RTGS / IMPS Bank Transfer, Razorpay Links.
- **Overdue Interest:** Payments past due by more than 7 days accrue 1.5% interest per month.
