01. Executive Sales Playbook & Discovery SOP

Purpose: Standardize KamiyTech's B2B sales engine, lead qualification framework, discovery diagnostic scripts, objection handling matrix, closing strategies, and sales-to-operations handoff SOP.

Owner: Head of Sales & Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

Version: 2.0.0 (APPROVED / LIVE)

Effective Date: July 2026

1. B2B Sales Funnel Architecture

KamiyTech operates a consultative 7-stage B2B sales pipeline:

  1. Lead Inbound / Outbound Prospecting: Initial lead capture and 4-hour SLA response.
  2. Lead Qualification Sync (15 Mins): Fast BANT/MEDDIC qualification.
  3. Deep Diagnostic Discovery Call (45 Mins): In-depth operational and technical pain point analysis.
  4. Proposal & Solution Presentation (45 Mins): Live walkthrough of custom technical proposal.
  5. Scope Alignment & Commercial Negotiation: Finalizing scope parameters and SOW terms.
  6. Contract Execution & Initial Deposit: Digital signature and 40%–50% deposit invoice clearing.
  7. Formal Sales-to-Ops Handoff: Briefing documentation and client kickoff meeting.

2. Lead Qualification Framework (Adapted BANT/MEDDIC)

Sales reps must qualify all prospects against 5 Core Filters during the 15-minute qualification sync:

  • Budget Alignment: Minimum deal sizes: Landing Pages (₹12k–₹15k), Web Platforms (₹35k–₹2.2L), Mobile Apps & SaaS (₹1.2L–₹10L+), AI Automations (₹45k–₹4.5L).
  • Authority: Direct access to C-suite economic decision-makers (Founder, CEO, Managing Director, COO).
  • Need & Urgency: Operational bottleneck or launch target within 30 to 90 days.
  • Technical Fit: Next.js, React, Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React Native, AI APIs.
  • Cultural Fit: Quality-oriented, collaborative clients valuing structured process over bargain pricing.

3. Discovery Call Script & SOP (45 Mins)

Diagnostic Question Framework

"Can you walk me through your current process for inventory tracking / client onboarding? Where do the biggest manual bottlenecks or delays happen today, and what is this costing your business each month?"

Key areas covered include: Operational Pain Points, Technical Systems & API Sync, Business Impact Metrics, Target Launch Timelines, and Board Decision Process.

4. Objection Handling Matrix

Common Objection Strategic Reframe Recommended Response Script
"Your price is higher than cheap freelancers." Reframe from cost to risk reduction & engineering standards. "We understand you can find low-cost template builders. However, KamiyTech operates as an enterprise technology consultancy—we provide senior architecture, strict type safety, zero technical debt, and long-term support. Fixing broken template code down the road costs 2x to 3x more."
"Can we start with a smaller test project first?" Validate risk mitigation; offer modular Phase 1 scoping. "We welcome starting small. We can begin with a single-page landing website (₹12,000–₹15,000) or Phase 1 Architecture Blueprint (₹35,000) to test our engineering speed and design quality with zero long-term commitment."
"We need to discuss this with our board." Offer custom executive presentation sync. "That makes complete sense. Let's schedule a 30-minute Proposal Review Sync for next Tuesday. I will prepare a clean executive summary deck covering ROI, architecture, and milestones for your board."

5. Closing Techniques & Pipeline Rules

  • Mutual Action Plan (MAP): Never email proposals blindly without a live walkthrough meeting.
  • 48-Hour SLA Follow-Up: Every active CRM opportunity must have a scheduled task within 48 hours.
  • Closing Checklist: Contract digitally executed and 40-50% deposit cleared by CFO before engineering kickoff.
