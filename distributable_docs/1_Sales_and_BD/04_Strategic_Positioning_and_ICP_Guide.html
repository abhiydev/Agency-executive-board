04. Strategic Positioning & ICP Guide

Purpose: Establish KamiyTech's official market positioning, Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs), target decision-maker personas, Unique Selling Proposition (USP) matrix, and sales messaging frameworks.

Owner: Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) & CEO

Version: 2.0.0 (APPROVED / LIVE)

1. Executive Positioning Statement

"KamiyTech is a full-service technology consultancy that designs, builds, and automates high-performance digital products for growth-focused businesses, modernizing operations through custom software, web platforms, mobile applications, and enterprise AI."

2. Target Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

ICP 1: SMBs & Growing Companies

Characteristics: ₹1 Cr – ₹25 Cr revenue, 10–100 employees, operating with legacy manual processes or outdated websites.

Key Decision Maker: Founder, Managing Director, COO, VP Operations.

KamiyTech Solution: Custom web portals, automated workflows, mobile field apps, integrated database management.

ICP 2: Tech Startups & Product Founders

Characteristics: Seed to Series A funded, fast product roadmaps, lean internal tech team.

Key Decision Maker: Founder, CEO, CTO, Head of Product.

KamiyTech Solution: Dedicated engineering pods (Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL), AI feature integration, rapid MVP builds.

ICP 3: Traditional Industries (Manufacturing, Logistics, Real Estate)

Characteristics: Operations-heavy businesses with legacy ERP/CRM reliance, paper-based tracking.

Key Decision Maker: General Manager, VP Supply Chain, Operations Lead.

KamiyTech Solution: Custom web/mobile dashboards, IoT/API integrations, automated inventory & workflow engines.
