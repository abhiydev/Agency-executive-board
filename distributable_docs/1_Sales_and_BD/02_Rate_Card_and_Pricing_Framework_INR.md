## 5. Commercial Terms & Billing Governance

### Payment Milestone Standards
- **Single-Page Landing Pages (₹12,000 – ₹15,000):**
  - **Milestone 1:** 50% Advance Deposit upon contract signing.
  - **Milestone 2:** 50% Final Payment upon pre-launch review & code deployment.
- **Fixed-Price Projects (Web, Mobile, AI):**
  - **Milestone 1:** 40% Advance Deposit upon SOW execution & project kickoff.
  - **Milestone 2:** 30% Staging Demo Signoff (upon feature-complete UAT demo).
  - **Milestone 3:** 30% Final Pre-Launch Handover (prior to DNS transfer & code release).

### Commercial Rules & Statutory Compliance
1. **GST Itemization:** All prices quoted in proposals are exclusive of 18% Goods and Services Tax (GST). Official GST tax invoices are issued for every payment trigger.
2. **Payment Modes:** Accepted payment mechanisms include UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm), NEFT / RTGS / IMPS Direct Bank Transfer, and Razorpay Corporate Payment Links.
3. **Late Payment Penalty:** Invoices unpaid after 7 calendar days accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month until settled.
4. **Out-of-Scope Work:** Any change request outside the approved SOW is estimated using the Hourly Role Rate Card (§2) and requires a formal Change Order execution.
