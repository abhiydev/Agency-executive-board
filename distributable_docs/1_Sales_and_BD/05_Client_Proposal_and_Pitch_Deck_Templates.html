05. Proposal & Pitch Deck Templates

Purpose: Standardize client proposal formats, executive pitch deck layouts, commercial quotation templates, and Statement of Work (SOW) attachments for KamiyTech sales operations.

Owner: Creative Director, Head of Sales & CFO

Version: 2.0.0 (APPROVED / LIVE)

1. Master Proposal Layout Structure

  1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives: High-level project summary and business outcome targets.
  2. Business Problem & Opportunity Analysis: Detailed diagnostic context and solution rationale.
  3. Proposed Technical Architecture: Stack specification (Next.js, React, Node.js/FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Vercel/AWS).
  4. Scope of Deliverables & Features: Phased execution breakdown (UX/UI, Engineering, QA & Staging).
  5. Milestone Timeline & Roadmap: Sprints and release schedules.
  6. Commercial Investment & Payment Terms: Fixed-price investment table and 40/30/30 or 50/50 payment triggers.
  7. Acceptance & Execution Signoff: Executive signature block.

2. Master 10-Slide Pitch Deck Structure

Slide Number Slide Title Core Slide Content & Objective
Slide 1 Title Slide KamiyTech Logo, Client Name, Proposal Title, Executive Date.
Slide 2 Executive Context Market challenge context & strategic alignment pillars.
Slide 3 Core Business Problem Highlighting operational friction, technical debt, & manual delays.
Slide 4 The KamiyTech Solution Enterprise web app, mobile suite, and AI automation overview.
Slide 5 Technical Architecture Visual stack diagram: Next.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Cloud Infra.
Slide 6 Scope Breakdown 3-phase execution roadmap from service catalog.
Slide 7 Case Studies & Proven ROI Quantified metrics (-35% errors, <1.5s load, 50% faster onboarding).
Slide 8 Agile Delivery Pod Senior engineering oversight & sprint execution model.
Slide 9 Commercial Investment Transparent milestone investment tiers & retainer plans.
Slide 10 Call to Action Mutual Action Plan (MAP) & kickoff sprint reservation.
